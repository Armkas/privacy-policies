## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**1. Why can't I send more than 5 messages to a new match?**
To ensure a safe and respectful environment for everyone, Natify has a built-in anti-harassment system. If the other person hasn't replied to your messages, you are limited to sending a maximum of 5 consecutive messages. Once they reply, you can chat freely!

**2. How does the offline AI translation work?**
Natify utilizes an advanced on-device LLM (Large Language Model) that runs entirely offline on your phone. This means your translation requests are processed locally, ensuring ultra-fast results, zero network dependency, and 100% privacy protection.

**3. How do I report an inappropriate user?**
Trust and safety are our top priorities. You can report or block a user directly from their profile page or within the chat screen. Our moderation team will review the report and take appropriate action.

**4. How do I delete my account and data?**
You can permanently delete your account and all associated data by going to **Settings → Account → Delete Account** within the app.

## 🔒 Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 21, 2026
**Last Updated:** August 21, 2026

Natify ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we collect it, how we use it, and with whom we share it when you use the Natify mobile application ("the App").

### 1. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of data to provide and improve our services:

| Category | Examples | How Collected |
|---|---|---|
| **Account Information** | Email address, display name, profile photo, native language, languages you are learning | Provided by you during registration and profile setup |
| **Device Information** | Device model, operating system version, app version, push notification tokens (APNs/VoIP) | Collected automatically from your device |
| **Chat & Communication Data** | Text messages, images shared in chat, voice/video call metadata (duration, timestamps) | Generated when you use the chat and calling features |
| **Usage Data** | Feature usage patterns, app foreground/background status, last active timestamps | Collected automatically during app usage |
| **Translation Input Data** | Text you submit for translation or AI-assisted features | Provided by you when using translation, AI roleplay, or icebreaker generation features |
| **Purchase Data** | Subscription status, transaction receipts | Provided by Apple via In-App Purchase |

### 2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect for the following purposes:

- **Providing Core Services:** Matching you with language partners, enabling text/image/voice/video communication, and managing your account.
- **AI-Powered Features:** Processing your text input through AI services to provide translations, grammar corrections, AI conversation roleplay, and icebreaker topic generation.
- **Notifications:** Sending you push notifications for new messages, match updates, and call invitations.
- **Safety & Moderation:** Enforcing our community guidelines, preventing harassment, and responding to user reports.
- **Service Improvement:** Analyzing aggregated, anonymized usage patterns to improve app features and performance.

### 3. Third-Party AI Data Sharing

**This section is critical: Natify uses third-party AI services to power certain premium features.**

When you use the following features, **your text input is sent to a third-party AI service for processing**:

| Feature | Data Sent | Third-Party Provider |
|---|---|---|
| **Online Translation** (Premium) | The text you request to translate | **DeepSeek** (a third-party AI service) |
| **AI Roleplay Conversation** (Premium) | Your conversation messages and the scenario context | **DeepSeek** |
| **Icebreaker Topic Generation** (Premium) | Optional context text you provide | **DeepSeek** |
| **AI Chat Assistant** (Premium) | Your chat message to the bot | **DeepSeek** |

**Important clarifications:**
- **Explicit Consent Required:** The App will ask for your explicit permission via an in-app alert **before** any text data is sent to a third-party AI service. You can choose to decline.
- **Free Users Are Not Affected:** These cloud AI features are only available to paid subscribers. Free users' data is never sent to any third-party AI service. Free users can use on-device (offline) translation and the built-in iOS translation framework, which do not transmit data externally.
- **On-Device AI Is Fully Private:** If you choose the "LLM Translation" (offline) engine, all AI processing happens entirely on your device. No data leaves your phone.
- **No Model Training:** Your personal data is **not** used by DeepSeek or by us to train, improve, or fine-tune any AI models.
- **Data Minimization:** We only send the specific text input required to generate the response. We do not send your name, email, profile details, or any other personal information to the AI provider.

### 4. Third-Party Services (Non-AI)

In addition to AI services, we use the following third-party services to operate the App:

| Service | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Backend database, authentication, real-time messaging relay | Account data, chat metadata |
| **Agora** | Real-time voice and video calling (RTC) | Audio/video streams during calls |
| **Apple (APNs)** | Push notifications | Device push tokens |

These service providers are bound by their own privacy policies and data processing agreements. They are contractually required to protect your data and only use it for the purposes of providing services to Natify.

### 5. Data Retention

- **Account Data:** Retained for as long as your account is active. Deleted upon account deletion request.
- **Chat Messages:** Text messages are stored locally on your device using Core Data. They are not stored on our servers beyond the real-time relay.
- **AI Processing Data:** Text sent to DeepSeek for processing is not permanently stored by DeepSeek or by us after the response is generated.

### 6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

- **Access & Portability:** You can view your profile data within the App at any time.
- **Correction:** You can update your profile information at any time through the App.
- **Deletion:** You can permanently delete your account and all associated data by going to **Settings → Account → Delete Account**. This action is irreversible.
- **Withdraw Consent:** You can withdraw your consent for cloud AI data sharing at any time by switching to the "System Translation" or "LLM Translation" (offline) engine in Translation Settings.

### 7. Children's Privacy

Natify is not intended for children under the age of 17. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 17. If we discover that we have collected data from a child under 17, we will delete it promptly.

### 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or via email. Your continued use of Natify after such updates constitutes your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
