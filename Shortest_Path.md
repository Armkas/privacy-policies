## 1. Description of the Service

The App provides computational path-sequencing and route-planning utilities designed to assist users in organizing multiple destination stops. The App utilizes mathematical algorithms (such as Traveling Salesperson Problem heuristics) to suggest theoretical orderings and distances. Features include:
* Multi-point location marking and automatic stop re-sequencing.
* Trip status tracking (marking stops as "Visited" or "Pending").
* Handoff and deep-linking to external third-party navigation providers (such as Apple Maps or Google Maps).
