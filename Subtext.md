## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**1. How does the offline AI analysis work?**
Subtext utilizes an advanced on-device LLM (Large Language Model) powered by the **Qwen 3.5** series. It runs entirely offline on your iPhone. The app smartly detects your device's memory limits and automatically downloads the most appropriate model size to ensure a balance of speed and high-quality responses—all with zero network dependency and 100% privacy protection.

**2. Why is the AI taking a long time to generate a reply?**
Because the AI runs entirely locally, generation speed depends on your iPhone's hardware. The app automatically selects the best model for your device's RAM, but devices with an A14 Bionic chip or newer will experience the fastest performance. Older devices may take a few extra seconds to process.

**3. Do I need to create an account or connect to the internet?**
No! Subtext requires absolutely no account registration. After the initial one-time download of the AI model, the app can run entirely in airplane mode. We do not track, collect, or upload your private conversations to any servers. Your secrets remain strictly yours.

**4. What languages does Subtext support?**
The AI automatically detects and adapts to your device's system language. Whether you want to analyze messages in English, Simplified/Traditional Chinese, or Spanish, the app will generate culturally relevant responses in your native language.
