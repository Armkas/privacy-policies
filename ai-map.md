# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 3, 2026

Thank you for using **AI-map** (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our services.

By using the App, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

AI-map is designed to provide AI-powered map interaction and navigation features while minimizing the amount of personal information we collect.

The App does not require you to create or sign in to an account provided by AI-map. We do not require your name, email address, phone number, or password to use the App.

### A. Location Data (GPS)

* **What we collect:** The App may request access to your device's precise location data.
* **How we use it:** Location data is used to provide map rendering, navigation, location-based features, and location-related AI responses.
* **Storage:** We do not maintain a personal location history on our servers.
* **Third-Party Integration:** The App uses Apple's MapKit and related Apple mapping services. Location data processed by Apple's services is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

For more information, please refer to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### B. Voice and Audio Data (Microphone)

* **What we collect:** The App may request microphone access to enable voice commands. Audio is captured only when you explicitly use the voice interaction features, such as "Push-to-Talk" or an active "Free-Chat" session.
* **How we use it:** Voice input is used to transcribe your commands into text so that the App can execute map commands and interact with the AI.
* **Storage:** We do not permanently store your voice recordings on our servers.
* **Background Recording:** The App does not secretly record audio in the background without your knowledge or an active interaction with the voice features.

### C. Text Input

* **What we collect:** The App processes text that you voluntarily enter into the chat interface.
* **How we use it:** Your text input is used to execute map commands, answer questions, and generate AI responses.
* **Storage:** We do not permanently store your chat conversations on our servers unless otherwise explicitly stated in the App.

### D. AI Credits and In-App Purchase Information

The App may offer consumable in-app purchases, such as AI Credits, that can be used to access AI-powered features.

* **Payment Processing:** All purchases are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive or store your credit card number, bank account information, Apple Account password, or other payment credentials.
* **Purchase Verification:** The App may process information associated with an Apple in-app purchase transaction in order to verify the purchase and grant the corresponding Credits.
* **Credits Balance:** The current balance of your AI Credits is maintained by the App. The balance is separate from Apple's payment records and is not itself an Apple payment account balance.
* **No AI-map Account Required:** You do not need to create an AI-map account in order to purchase or use Credits.

## 2. iCloud and Cloud Data Synchronization

To provide a better experience across Apple devices, the App may use Apple's iCloud and/or CloudKit services to store and synchronize certain App data.

### A. What May Be Stored in iCloud

When iCloud is available on your device, certain App data may be stored or synchronized using Apple's iCloud services, such as:

* Your AI Credits balance;
* Information necessary to maintain the state of your Credits;
* Certain App settings or preferences required for synchronization.

The specific information synchronized may change as the App develops.

### B. iCloud Account

The App does **not** create a separate AI-map user account.

If you are signed in to iCloud on your Apple device, the App may use your existing Apple iCloud account to access the App's iCloud or CloudKit data.

The App does not require you to provide us with your Apple Account email address, password, or other Apple Account credentials.

For CloudKit private databases, Apple provides account-based access control so that private data belongs to the user's iCloud account and is accessible only to that user by default.

For more information about Apple's handling of iCloud data, please refer to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### C. If iCloud Is Not Available

If you are not signed in to iCloud, or if iCloud is otherwise unavailable, the App may keep applicable App data, including your AI Credits balance, locally on your device.

In this situation:

> **Credits stored only on the device may not be recoverable if you replace your device, delete the App, lose the device, or lose the locally stored App data.**

We recommend enabling iCloud on your device so that supported App data can be synchronized across your Apple devices.

The App does not require iCloud in order to use the App or make purchases unless explicitly stated otherwise in the App.

## 3. Third-Party AI Service Providers

To process your text and transcribed voice commands and provide AI-powered functionality, the App uses third-party Large Language Model (LLM) service providers through APIs.

* **Data Shared:** The App may send the text of your commands or queries to these AI providers in order to generate responses.
* **Location Information:** The App does not intentionally send your exact GPS coordinates to third-party AI providers unless the information is explicitly included in the text sent for processing or is otherwise necessary for a requested feature.
* **Personal Information:** We do not intentionally attach your name, email address, Apple Account credentials, payment card information, or other directly identifying personal information to requests sent to third-party AI providers.
* **Use of Data:** We use these providers to process requests and generate AI responses necessary for the App's functionality.
* **Data Retention:** The App itself does not permanently store your AI conversations on our servers. However, third-party providers may temporarily process or retain request data according to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Where technically and contractually applicable, we take reasonable steps to use AI service providers that provide appropriate protections for user data.

## 4. Data Retention and Storage

We follow a data-minimization approach and do not maintain a permanent server-side history of your personal use of the App.

### Information We Do Not Permanently Store on Our Servers

We do not intentionally maintain permanent server-side records of:

* Your location history;
* Your voice recordings;
* Your complete chat history;
* Your Apple Account password;
* Your payment card information.

Voice data may be processed transiently for transcription.

Text commands may be processed in real time in order to provide the requested AI functionality.

### Local and iCloud Storage

Certain information may be stored locally on your device.

If iCloud is available, supported App data such as AI Credits and selected settings may also be synchronized using Apple's iCloud/CloudKit services.

The availability and synchronization of this data may depend on your Apple device, iCloud account status, network connection, and Apple's services.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13, or the equivalent minimum age in the applicable jurisdiction.

If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information in violation of applicable law, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

Data stored through Apple's iCloud or CloudKit services is subject to Apple's security and privacy mechanisms and policies.

For information about Apple's security practices, please refer to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

The App may use third-party services necessary to provide its functionality, including:

Disabling certain permissions or services may prevent some App features from functioning correctly.
