# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 2, 2026

Thank you for using our application ("AI-map"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our services. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

To provide our core AI-driven navigation and map control features, the App requires access to specific device permissions and data:

### A. Location Data (GPS)

- **What we collect:** We request access to your device's precise location data.
- **How we use it:** Location data is strictly used to provide map rendering, navigation, and location-based AI responses.
- **Third-Party Integration:** We use Apple Maps (MapKit) as our mapping service provider. Your location data is processed in accordance with [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### B. Voice and Audio Data (Microphone)

- **What we collect:** The App requests microphone access to enable voice commands. Audio is only captured when you explicitly interact with the AI via "Push-to-Talk" mode or when you activate the continuous "Free-Chat" mode during an active session. 
- **How we use it:** Your voice input is used solely to transcribe your commands into text to control the map and interact with the AI. The App does not secretly record audio in the background without your knowledge.

### C. Text Input

- **What we collect:** Any text you type directly into the App's chat interface.
- **How we use it:** Text input is processed to execute map commands and generate AI responses.

## 2. Third-Party AI Service Providers

To process your text and transcribed voice commands intelligently, the App utilizes secure third-party Large Language Model (LLM) Service Providers via API. 

- **Data Shared:** Only the text of your commands or queries is sent to these third-party AI providers. 
- **Data Protection:** We do not attach your personal identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, or exact GPS coordinates (unless explicitly included in your prompt) to the data sent to the AI providers. We strictly prohibit our AI partners from using your chat data to train their public models.

## 3. Data Retention

We do not store your location history, voice recordings, or chat logs on our servers. 

- Voice data is processed transiently for transcription.
- Text commands are processed in real-time and are not permanently logged by us. 
- Your settings and preferences are stored locally on your device.

## 4. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
